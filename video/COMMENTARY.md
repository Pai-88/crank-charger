## One take, plain text

A hand crank phone charger. Every plastic part is printed, the gearbox in one piece.
Inside: a planetary stage. Seventeen, thirty-seven and ninety-one teeth, five point three to one.
Three planets on steel pins, one sun on the motor shaft. The motor is a stepper, run backwards as a generator.
Behind the wall: eight Schottky diodes in two bridges turn the stepper's output into DC, and one capacitor smooths it.
A buck converter brings it down to USB five volts, with a plug-in meter to show what the phone gets.
Cover on, phone on. Turn the wheel.
